SequencePang3 Coconut Island audio pack v1
Created 2026-10-04.

All musical scores in this pack were newly composed for the coconut 30-stage
untimed sequence-mahjong game. All sounds were synthesized deterministically
from mathematical waveforms and seeded noise. No sampled recordings, soundfont,
external song, commercial music-generation service or third-party stem was used.
Synthesis and numerical QA utilities are adapted from the earlier original
SequencePang2 pack made for this user; its compositions and recordings were not
reused. New tine-like, felt-key and coconut-tone voices were added here.

Instrument names describe synthesized timbres, not performers or real recordings.
The pack includes code and score data for editing and recreation. No assertion
of unique legal copyrightability, guaranteed non-similarity to every existing
song, or human-listening/mastering approval is made.

Technical checks are scoped to files and their numerical signals. The transition
demo is assembled offline, not recorded from an integrated game.
The repository and live deployment were not modified by this asset task.
